About

I build real systems — and I care about understanding what happens underneath them.

I’m Jonathan, a full-stack engineer based in Cairo. I build production web and mobile applications across the frontend, backend, database, and infrastructure layers. My work has increasingly focused on data-heavy and enterprise systems, where architecture, data modeling, authentication, performance, and maintainability matter just as much as getting the features working.

I currently work on Qodum, a large school ERP platform, where I’m involved in modernizing the system, migrating its data layer from MongoDB to PostgreSQL with Prisma, improving application architecture, and addressing the challenges that come with a growing production application.

I’ve also built applications from the ground up, including Gursha, a social media platform with a React Native client and a Node.js backend. Working on these systems has made me increasingly interested in the engineering problems behind the applications themselves — databases, system architecture, performance, networking, and scalability.

Outside of the applications I build, I’m deliberately going deeper into computer science fundamentals. I want to understand not just how to build software, but why the systems underneath it work the way they do.
